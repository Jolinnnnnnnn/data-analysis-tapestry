## Iteration 5 (precision + cache)
- Goal: fix wrong display rounding found on the Build page (naive YoY showed +7.4%, FINDINGS says +7.5%).
- Cause: metrics.json stored fractions at 4 dp, so 1-dp percentages sit on rounding ties (0.0745 → 7.4 or 7.5?). L1W W29 0.1265 rendered +12.6% vs the audited +12.7%.
- Actions: `r()` in 11_metrics.py → 6 dp (D19). Separately, `st.cache_data` ignored the `_mtime` argument (leading-underscore params are not hashed), so a rebuilt metrics.json was never re-read; renamed to `mtime`.
- Result: findings audit 30/30; anomalies unchanged (71; 8 critical); the validator now flagged **7** numbers in my session texts that had been rounded on 4-dp ties (W23 28.9→28.8% twice, W24 +9.2→+9.1%, W27 -8.1→-8.0% twice, W28 37.8→37.7%, W29 +12.2→+12.1%). Corrected, re-validated, re-stamped (PROMPTS P20).
- Validator tightened: week-index fields (`weeks`, `wk`) are excluded from the fact leaves, because the trace showed "28" (a count) matching a week number.
